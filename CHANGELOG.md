- Publish a clean, parentless research-only release.

- Preserve contrast direction, reject duplicate semantic aliases, and distinguish bundled provenance from same-named uploads.
- Add an explicitly synthetic 16-row demonstration fixture.
- Add native tests, public-tree and full-history gates, release validation, CI, Pages, and dependency automation.
